**Paste your website. Get scroll-stopping content in minutes.**

MultiCast is the automated marketing engine for modern brands. Drop in your website once and it learns your brand — colors, voice, audience — then turns it into hook-first carousels, short videos, feed images, and text posts, plans them into campaigns, and publishes them to your channels on schedule. You just give the OK.

Faster than an agency, more consistent than a freelancer, and always on-brand — starting at $29 a month. It's built for solo founders, small business owners, creators, and small teams — anyone who is their own marketer and would rather hand the work off than hire for it.

## What it does

- **Learns your brand from one link.** Enter your website or Instagram handle and MultiCast builds your Brand Kit: logo, colors, fonts, voice, and audience. Every piece of content starts there, so nothing drifts off-tone.
- **Makes content that hooks.** Carousels, short videos, feed images, and text posts designed to stop the scroll — no prompts to write, no design tools to learn.
- **Creates and runs campaigns.** Give it a theme and it makes weeks of content, shapes it into a campaign, and lines it up on a single calendar.
- **Publishes to every channel.** Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, X, and LinkedIn. The same content is fitted to each channel's format and goes out at the time you set.
- **Handles comments and DMs.** Set a keyword on a post and everyone who comments it gets an automatic DM. AI replies answer from your brand knowledge, and hand off to you when they're not sure. Available on Instagram.
- **Shows what's working.** Results at a glance: which formats, times, and posts perform, and what to make next.
